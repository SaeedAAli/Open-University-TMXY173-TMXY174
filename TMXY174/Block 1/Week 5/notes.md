# MOSCOW Scaling

MOSCOW is a prioritisation technique to assess how critical the situation is and how fast is it needed to be approached

* Must Have - Non-Negotiable, this is priority number one in terms of it needing it to be addressed and acted fast upon
* Should Have - Important, high value requirements that are not critical for the immediate launch, 
* Could Have - Desirable, Nice to have features that can improve the current project if permissible by asking other engineers and people above your seniority
* Won't Have - Doesn't fit the scope of the current project and furthermore it isn't something that is needed to implement; think of it as having an idea on the back of your mind
