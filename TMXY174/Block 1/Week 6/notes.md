# 6.1 What does ethics mean in project management
When it comes to ethical in project management, its the moral principle that guide decisions and behaviour, helping to make sure that is carried out fairly and responsibility

As a project management, the foundation principles is 
* **Integrity** - The principle of acting consistently with ethical and professional standards.
* **Accountability** - taking responsibility for decisions
* **respect** - recognizing the value of a person or thing and treating them with care, dignity, and kindness
* **confidentiality and data protection** - ownership of your product
* **transparency** -  acing openly and providing clear information about decisions

**Ethical Dilemmas** is a situation in which competing goals on value create uncertainty about appropriate action.
    -> identify the issue and what value are at stake
    -> conduct a COC or mentor assess potential consequences
    -> Choose the action that upholds integrity


# 6.2 Sustainability in project management

What is **sustainability**: It's an approach that meets present needs without compromising the future for other people to meet their own needs

Triple bottom line - Triple P
Planet - this is the environmental factor; is it suitable to conduct something that will do no harm in terms of the species factor like animals, people etc
People - does it benefit the community as something that has value for a long period of time
Profit - is this idea made fairly and maintains responsibility and morale


# 6.2.1 Sustainable practices in project management

* **Green IT** is reducing energy use to reduce the environmental footprint associated with hardware and infrastructure
        -> an example for this would be to recycle or re use hardware that may have some benefit towards the objective / goals that you're achieving rather than purchasing new material which contribute to more lead and other scientific elements being used to make laptops, computers and more

* **Ethical procurement** - selection and management of suppliers in ways that align with an organisation ethical and sustainability objectives.
does it match with the companies policies, and morals that you've established and not overlooking its laws, lawsuits and people opinions and thoughts when working at corporations / companies
    -> IOI = making sure that you're integrating it in your Vendor End Agreement (VEA)
             Org's successfully learned who to deal business in terms of are they who they say they are and are the following policies to the matter

* **Cloud Efficiency** - selecting energy - hosting options and reducing power consumptions
    -> an great example of this would be that when selecting a Virtual machine on GCP, it recommends you; depending on the size of your application, recommend the correct size in terms of RAM / Memory  and CPU cores that will be used. this is done to reduce carbon dioxide emissions and it's eco friendliness. Rather than selecting the highest CPU cores and RAM gigabytes available


**LifeCycle thinking**: think of it as building systems that are easy to maintain, re use and evolve
    -> approaching it like a 'lego like' mindset swapping components and maintenance plus very exclusive, well documented program/application. this is done to avoid rebuilding the entire infrastructure if someone are unable to follow the documentation that is provided to them



**Inclusive Design** In this case, its spreading your technology across 10's of billions of people and enabling the factor that no one is left out, social exclusion which improves the market and target demographic and the allows massive corporations stock portfolio to flourish and encourage investors to keep putting money in companies like sony, microsoft,NVIDIA, AMD and much more

its essentially maximising market reach and accessibility must be treated as a foundational requirement from the very first product iteration. The core objective is to deeply understand diverse user needs and design accordingly, e.g new website, more freedom to accommodate user needs than developing software that must integrate with legacy systems.

**Best example of this is** XBOX,Playstation,GPU,CPU,RAM Gaming PCS
